Employee scheduling

Crew schedule by line: every day one crew works the day shift and one crew works the night shift (times from the shift table PLR_SM, e.g. 08:00-20:00 and 20:00-08:00). Usually every crew works Day - Night - Sleep day - Day off. Generate fills the schedule with this rotation (every line needs 4 crews); pinned shifts are kept. An orange outline marks a shift with too little rest before it (hover for details; only a warning).

Add: double-click an empty place on a line at the time of the day or night shift. Change the crew: double-click a shift. Move: drag a shift. Remove: select a shift and press ×. Pin: click on a shift; a pinned shift cannot be moved or removed. People: click the crew name or on a shift to see and change the people of this one shift (the crew does not change). Click a crew in the crew list below to change the crew for all days.

By employee: a crew shift has the employees of its crew, but one shift can differ. Double-click an employee's shift to take them off this shift or replace them with a free employee (dashed = substitute, grey = absent). Double-click an empty place in an employee's row to add them to a crew shift at that time. Only that shift changes, not the crew. Click an employee's name to enter a vacation, sick leave or dismissal, or to move them to another crew from a date (red background = absence).

Crews

Click a crew (here, or its name in the "By employee" timeline) to see its employees (the people of its shifts). The crew column changes the crew of an employee for the whole month; click the employee's name for a crew change from a date, vacation, sick leave or dismissal. Changes are kept only in your schedule version.
