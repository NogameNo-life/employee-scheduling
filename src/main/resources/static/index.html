Employee scheduling

Crew schedule by line: every day one crew works the day shift (08:00-20:00) and one crew works the night shift (20:00-08:00). Usually every crew works Day - Night - Sleep day - Day off.

Add: double-click an empty place on a line (08:00-20:00 = day shift, 20:00-08:00 = night shift). Change the crew: double-click a shift. Move: drag a shift. Remove: select a shift and press ×. Pin: click on a shift; a pinned shift cannot be moved or removed. People: click the crew name or on a shift to see and change the people of this one shift (the crew does not change). Click a crew in the crew list below to change the crew for all days.

By employee: a crew shift has the employees of its crew, but one shift can differ. Double-click an employee's shift to take them off this shift or replace them with a free employee (dashed = substitute, grey = absent). Double-click an empty place in an employee's row to add them to a crew shift at that time. Only that shift changes, not the crew. Click an employee's name to enter a vacation, sick leave or dismissal, or to move them to another crew from a date (red background = absence).

Crews

Click a crew (here, or its name in the "By employee" timeline) to see its roles and its employees (the default composition of its shifts). The crew column changes the crew of an employee for the whole month; click the employee's name for a crew change from a date, vacation, sick leave or dismissal. Roles are read-only. Changes are kept only in your schedule version.
