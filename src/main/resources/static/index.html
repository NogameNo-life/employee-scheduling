Employee scheduling

Crew schedule by line: every day one crew works the day shift (08:00-20:00) and one crew works the night shift (20:00-08:00). Usually every crew works Day - Night - Sleep day - Day off.

Add: double-click an empty place on a line (08:00-20:00 = day shift, 20:00-08:00 = night shift). Change the crew: double-click a shift. Move: drag a shift. Remove: select a shift and press ×. Pin: click on a shift; a pinned shift cannot be moved or removed.

Crews

Click a crew (here, or its name in the timeline) to see and change its composition (people per shift for each role) and its employees. Changes are kept only in your schedule version.
